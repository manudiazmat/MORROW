# Surface brief — MORROW app (all tabs)

Mode: Operate. Direction pinned by the brief and the user's references (no concept-seed roll: `seed: none (brief-pinned)`). Taste app style: `editorial-greeting` (seed 1380624635), brand palette and type kept.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm personal home that answers "what is today, what do I do, how is my life moving" in that order. Refuses the KPI-tile dashboard: figures are type on tonal panels, and the only drawing that leads is the Life Balance flower.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory ground, sand/sage tonal panels, ink near-black primary. Depth only on what you touch (soft dual shadow on nav pill, FAB, buttons, segmented thumbs; inset wells for inputs, like the references). Panels are flat tone steps. Manrope, regular-weight tabular figures, one bold job (money). Line icons 1.5px. Dark: graphite with lightness steps, sage accent.

STORY: Greeting names the person and today's one fact from data → today's agenda and priorities → week (sport, money, goals) → the flower. Each block opens its module.

FIRST VIEWPORT: greeting h1 (sentence, 26px) with date line; "Hoy" panel with next event + pending count; priorities list; flower summary peeks.

SIGNATURE: the 8-petal flower — organic rounded annular sectors, petals grow from the hollow centre with a spring, tap lifts a petal and dims the rest.

RISK: neumorphism reduces contrast; text never relies on shadow, borders not used, tone steps carry structure.

Conflicts resolved: taste bans resting shadows/floating pill nav → the brief's neumorphism and the user's nav reference win, limited to interactive elements. Motion is owned by the Emil Kowalski system (springs, ease-out curves, ≤300ms UI), impeccable's Operate restraint (no orchestrated page loads).
